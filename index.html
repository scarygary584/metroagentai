FAQ

Questions business owners usually ask

Is this a human receptionist?

No. MetroAgentAI is an AI phone assistant. A real person answering is always best when available. MetroAgentAI is for calls that would otherwise be missed.

Can it take real orders?

It can collect order details and send a summary, but staff should review and confirm the request before preparing an order or accepting payment.

Can it use my menu or flower options?

Yes. The assistant can be configured with your common menu items, flower options, hours, service details, and preferred follow-up process.

What happens if it cannot answer?

It can collect the caller's question and contact details so your staff can follow up with the right answer.

Can I cancel?

Yes. The test-client offer is meant to be straightforward, with no long-term lock-in required unless a separate agreement is made.

Is this only for restaurants and florists?

No. Restaurants and florists are the first demos, but MetroAgentAI can also support local service businesses that miss calls while staff are busy.
