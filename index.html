Why this matters

Urgent callers do not wait long

When a customer has a leak, no AC, roof damage, broken glass, or another time-sensitive problem, they often call another company if nobody answers. Better intake can help capture the caller, problem, location, urgency, and callback details before the lead cools off.

FAQ

Questions about the calculators

Do these calculators guarantee revenue?

No. The calculators are informational only. They estimate missed opportunity from the numbers entered by the user.

Who are these tools for?

They are for local service businesses that miss calls while driving, working, sleeping, helping customers, or closing for the day.

Can these be used for other industries?

Yes. The same logic can be adapted for junk removal, septic, garage doors, water damage, locksmiths, and other call-driven local services.

What is MetroAgentAI?

MetroAgentAI is being built around AI phone assistant and missed-call tools for local service businesses.
