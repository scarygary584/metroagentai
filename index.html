FAQ

Questions about the calculator

Does this guarantee revenue?

No. The calculator is informational only. It estimates missed opportunity from the numbers entered by the user.

Why is this focused on plumbers?

Plumbing calls are often urgent, especially leaks, backups, no-hot-water calls, and after-hours problems. That makes missed calls easier to understand in dollars.

Can this be copied for other industries?

Yes. HVAC, roofing, auto glass, water damage, junk removal, and septic could all use similar missed-call calculators with adjusted wording and assumptions.

What is MetroAgentAI?

MetroAgentAI is being built around AI phone assistant and missed-call tools for local service businesses. The calculator is the first public resource.
